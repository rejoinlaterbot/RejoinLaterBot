# RejoinLaterBot privacy

The current policy for the official hosted `@RejoinLaterBot` service is maintained in
the repository's [Privacy Policy](../PRIVACY.md). Independent deployments and forks
must publish privacy information for their own users.

The official Service does not store group names, descriptions, avatars, group
messages, private invitation URLs, or Telegram profile names. For the complete list of
processed data, purposes, retention rules, service providers, and request process,
read the full policy.
