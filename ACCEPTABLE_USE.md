# Acceptable Use Policy for the official RejoinLaterBot

Last updated: August 15, 2026

This policy applies to the official hosted Telegram bot `@RejoinLaterBot` (the
**Service**). It does not change the Apache-2.0 license for the source code or impose
these hosted-service rules on independent deployments.

## Prohibited use

You must not use the Service:

- for unlawful activity or to facilitate, promote, conceal, or coordinate unlawful
  activity;
- to access or attempt to access a group, account, system, or data without
  authorization;
- to evade group bans, join restrictions, administrator decisions, Telegram
  enforcement, or other access controls;
- for spam, scams, phishing, malware, fraud, harassment, threats, stalking,
  exploitation, or abuse;
- to collect, infer, expose, or track personal data or group membership without a
  lawful basis and appropriate authorization;
- to impersonate another person, group, organization, the project maintainers, or an
  official deployment;
- to publish, sell, transfer, or misuse private invitation links;
- to probe, disrupt, overload, reverse engineer for abuse, or bypass security, rate
  limits, privacy controls, or operational restrictions of the Service;
- to automate abusive volumes of requests or interfere with the Service, Telegram, a
  group, or another user's use of either;
- in any way that violates Telegram's terms, applicable group rules, or the rights of
  others.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may investigate suspected violations and limit, suspend, or terminate
access to protect users, groups, Telegram, the Service, or the public. Where reasonably
necessary, information may be preserved or disclosed to comply with law, address a
security incident, or prevent harm, subject to the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

## Reporting abuse

Report suspected abuse through the
[project repository](https://github.com/rejoinlaterbot/RejoinLaterBot/issues) without
posting Telegram IDs, invite links, or other personal data. Ask for a private contact
channel when a report contains sensitive information. Report security vulnerabilities
through the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
